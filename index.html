Humility

Humility is a person's personal quality of being modest or humble with others unknown to them. This is a personal value that is very important for a person to have. Especially because it's a quality that not only reflects on us, but reflects on other people as well.

  • Importance

Apart from that, it basically makes us able to take in count other people's opinions, ideas, and beliefs. Humility also helps others feel more welcomed and less self-conscious about themselves.
